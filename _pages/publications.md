My published work spans functional porphyrins and BODIPY dyes, catalytic oxygen reduction and molecular chemosensing.
</div>

<div class="publication-list">

### Photodynamic antimicrobial activity of halogenated BODIPY dyes
**A. K. May, C. Chiyumba, J. Harris, J. Mack and T. Nyokong**  
*Journal of Porphyrins and Phthalocyanines* **2022**, 26, 691–700.

I designed the compound series and led its synthesis, structure–activity analysis and analytical characterisation.

### Proton-management substituents in cobalt-porphyrin oxygen-reduction catalysis
**Y. Wei, L. Zhao, R. Yuan, Z. Xue, J. Mack, C. Chiyumba, T. Nyokong and J. Zhang**  
*Inorganic Chemistry* **2022**, 61, 13085–13095.

I synthesised and characterised the cobalt-porphyrin catalyst series underpinning the study.

### Quinoline–thiophene Schiff-base chemosensor for mercury detection
**B. Musikavanhu, S. Muthusamy, D. Zhu, Z. Xue, Q. Yu, C. N. Chiyumba, J. Mack, T. Nyokong, S. Wang and L. Zhao**  
*Spectrochimica Acta Part A* **2022**, 264, 120338.

A collaborative study of a turn-off fluorescent chemosensor for Hg²⁺ detection.
