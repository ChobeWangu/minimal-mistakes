## Professional experience

Before graduate study, I worked with Zambia’s Ministry responsible for public infrastructure. As a Clerk of Works on a major conference-centre development, I supported quality control, standards compliance, reinforcement inspection, site testing and programme monitoring.

That experience continues to shape how I think: technical quality matters, but so do institutions, communication and the systems surrounding delivery.

## Selected achievements

- Presented research on porphyrin–protein conjugates and artificial metalloenzymes at scientific meetings.
- Recognised for communicating complex research to non-specialist audiences.
- Led interdisciplinary team-based problem solving during Cambridge professional-development programmes.
- Progressed through competitive university-level consideration for international science fellowships.

## Current direction

I am preparing for research opportunities in medicinal chemistry, drug discovery and global health, with a particular commitment to malaria. I am also open to interdisciplinary opportunities connecting science, sustainability and innovation.
