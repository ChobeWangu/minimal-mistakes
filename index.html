DOCTORAL RESEARCH

Artificial metalloenzymes for new-to-nature chemistry

My Cambridge PhD investigates synthetic Ir(III), Zn(II) and Co(II) porphyrin cofactors within engineered protein scaffolds. I designed, synthesised and characterised the molecular components, engineered protein variants, and built quantitative workflows to understand their assembly and behaviour.
