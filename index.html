THE IMPACT

Towards more sustainable chemical production

Chemical synthesis is essential to medicines and new technologies, yet many transformations still depend on harsh conditions, high energy inputs or processes that generate avoidable waste. I am interested in a different approach: combining the precision of synthetic organic chemistry with the selectivity of engineered biological systems.

Synthetic chemistry gives me control over molecular structure. Artificial metalloenzymes offer a route towards selective reactivity under mild, biologically compatible conditions. Together, these distinct research pillars support my long-term goal of helping develop cleaner and more efficient ways to make valuable molecules.
